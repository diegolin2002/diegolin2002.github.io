Lodr

收藏、分類、鎖住你的連結 · Save, sort and lock your links

Lodr 是一款簡潔的連結收藏工具。從任何 App 一鍵分享進來、分類整理、私密分類可上鎖，換手機也能備份與還原。
Lodr is a clean, simple link collector: share links in from any app, organize them into folders, lock private ones, and move to a new phone with cloud backup.
